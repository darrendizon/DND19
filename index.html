About The Infinite Horizon

Welcome to The Infinite Horizon, an accessible text-based adventure game. Explore procedurally generated worlds, battle monsters, and discover secrets.

This game features high contrast visual themes, screen reader support, and immersive soundscapes.

Use the controls to interact with the world. All actions are keyboard accessible.
